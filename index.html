Join a Game

How it works. Each group sets up a homestead (land, house, tools), then plays 5 years. Every year: plant in spring, face a chance card in summer based on real events (drought, grasshoppers, the Children's Blizzard, railroad rates, the Dawes Act), sell in the fall, and pay the bank in winter. At the end, each family either proves up, stays in debt, loses the farm, or goes back east.

About 30 minutes. Nothing to install and no student logins. Keep the board tab open during the game; it is the game. If your school network blocks the live connection, use No-Connection Mode on the board: every group plays on its own computer and you reveal each year's news with an unlock word.
